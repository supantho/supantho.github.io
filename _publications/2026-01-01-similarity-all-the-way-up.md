We show that the internal representations of multilingual language models implicitly recover the phylogenetic structure of the Indo-European language family. Using a token-first discretization of model representations, we derive pairwise language distances and compare the resulting trees against established reference phylogenies (Heggarty et al., 2023; Glottolog). Across 37 Indo-European languages, distances derived from XLM-RoBERTa align with the Heggarty reference tree at a path-kernel correlation of approximately 0.66. Crucially, the degree of alignment predicts cross-lingual generalization performance on XNLI, suggesting that the recovered structure is functionally meaningful rather than incidental.

*This paper received an oral presentation at CogSci 2026.*
